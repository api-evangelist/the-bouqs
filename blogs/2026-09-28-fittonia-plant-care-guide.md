With veins in pink, red, or silvery white running across deep green leaves, the fittonia is a beauty that can look hand-painted. It’s also called the nerve plant or mosaic plant, and it’s small enough for a desk, a bookshelf, or a terrarium. It has a reputation for being dramatic, since it will collapse in a heap the moment it gets thirsty.
