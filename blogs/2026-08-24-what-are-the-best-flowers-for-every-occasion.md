There’s a flower for nearly every feeling. A birthday calls for something bright and celebratory, a sympathy gift calls for something soft and comforting, and a “just because” bouquet can be as playful or as classic as you like. Picking the right blooms does more than fill a vase; it tells the recipient you thought about them specifically, not just the calendar date.
